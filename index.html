HOW IT WORKS

Toggle a channel ON — plays every new call on that channel automatically as it comes in, until you toggle it off.

Tap a channel's name — instantly plays its most recent call, without turning on continuous monitoring.

Calls on different channels play one at a time, in the order they arrive — if two overlap, the second one waits its turn.
